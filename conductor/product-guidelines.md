# Product Guidelines

## Documentation & Communication
- **Prose Style:** Technical and Concise. Focus on direct, to-the-point instructions and functional descriptions for documentation and commit messages.

## Visual & Interaction Design
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, Polished, and Minimalist. Utilize modern tools like WezTerm, Starship, and Fish with Nerd Fonts and descriptive icons, but maintain a clean interface by suppressing non-essential information (e.g., hiding CPU/RAM usage in WezTerm, keeping Starship prompts high-signal but low-noise).
- **Consistency:** Core-Shared, Edge-Unique. Maintain a unified experience for core aliases, themes, and behavior across Linux, WSL, and Windows to support muscle memory, while allowing platform-specific optimizations and enhancements where appropriate.

## Configuration Standards
- **Structure:** Modular and Documented. Break configurations into logical sub-files (e.g., separate Fish functions) and provide clear comments for complex logic or non-obvious settings to ensure long-term maintainability.
- **Evolution:** Iterative and Experimental. Add new tools and refine configurations through frequent, small, and iterative updates as workflow needs evolve.
